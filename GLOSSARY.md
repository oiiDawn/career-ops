# Career Ops

Career Ops evaluates job opportunities using evidence relevant to the role and the candidate's preferences.

## Language

**Company research**:
Research into an employer's business condition, working culture, and compensation as they apply to a particular opportunity. These three research dimensions are distinct from the opportunity's career direction.

**Applicable evidence**:
Source material that supports a claim about the relevant employer, role, location, level, team, and time period. Material with a different or uncertain scope must retain that limitation.

**Research finding**:
A sourced statement that distinguishes supported facts, inferences, and unresolved questions. An unsupported inference is not a verified fact.

**Research quality**:
The correctness, applicability, and coverage of findings, including their treatment of uncertainty. Fluency and agreement with an expected score do not establish research quality.

**Research summary**:
A concise account of findings that preserves their source references and material limitations. Summarizing must not turn uncertainty into certainty.

**Candidate preference**:
A personal priority or threshold used to score an opportunity's attractiveness. Preferences affect scoring, not the collection or description of public employer facts.
