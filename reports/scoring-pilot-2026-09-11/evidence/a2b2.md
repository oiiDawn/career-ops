**About A2B2.ai**

A2B2.ai provides active investors with an agentic personal office that manages assets using hybrid intelligence, combining extensive machine capabilities with informed human judgment. Focused on helping users capture both Alpha and Beta returns, A2B2.ai serves as a trusted, ongoing companion designed to compound wealth over the long term.

The platform maintains strict objectivity by refusing to sell financial products, offer investment advice, or accept commissions. Operating solely on a service-fee model, A2B2.ai prioritizes clarity, trust, and delivers data directly into the investor's workflow. This environment offers technology professionals the opportunity to build transparent, investor-first tools at the intersection of finance and AI.

**The Role**

We are looking for an AI Engineer with strong backend fundamentals to help build the intelligence layer behind A2B2.ai. You will work on the agent systems, retrieval pipelines, and backend services that turn wealth domain knowledge into reliable, working AI capabilities.

This is a hands-on building role. You will take ownership of features end to end - from prompt and tool design, through the Python services that run them, to the APIs that surface them to our members - with support and mentorship from senior engineers on architecture and design decisions.

Front-end work is not a core part of this role - a separate team owns the interface layer. You should be comfortable reading front-end code and making small changes when needed.

**What You Will Do**

**Agent & Intelligence Systems**

- Help build and operate the agentic workflows behind A2B2.ai - investment research, portfolio monitoring, risk assessment, and advisor workflows.
- Contribute to our model routing and multi-model cross-examination work, so frontier and specialist models check each other before anything is surfaced to a member.
- Build retrieval pipelines over institutional-grade sources, including hybrid semantic and metadata search.
- Work on the memory and context layer so the platform adapts to each user and improves with use.
**Backend Services & APIs**

- Build and maintain the Python services and REST APIs that power our AI capabilities.
- Model data properly - schema design, query optimization, and keeping performance predictable as the platform grows.
- Handle the operational side: async job processing, caching, rate limiting, error handling, and graceful degradation when models misbehave.
- Troubleshoot production issues, write clean and testable code, participate in code reviews, and contribute to documentation.
**Quality & Observability**

- Help build the tracing and observability tooling that keeps AI outputs credible and debuggable at scale.
- Contribute to the guardrails that hold AI behaviour to a real standard - a wealth intelligence platform lives or dies on the quality of its outputs.
- Enforce A2B2's non-advisory boundary in code: the platform makes members smarter and shows them everything before they decide; it never tells them what to buy or sell.

**Who You Are**

**Must Have**

- 0-3 years of professional experience. What matters is what you have built, not how long you have been working.
- Solid Python skills, with backend experience - you can build APIs, work with async, and model data sensibly. Python is our primary language and non-negotiable for this role.
- At least one real LLM application you have built - RAG, tool calling, or an agent system. It can be a personal project, open-source work, or something from an internship, but it should be something you built and can walk us through in depth, not a tutorial you followed.
- Working knowledge of PostgreSQL - you can design a reasonable schema and understand why a slow query is slow.
- Daily hands-on use of agentic coding tools. Claude and Codex should be part of how you work, not a novelty.
- Strong foundation in Computer Science concepts - data structures, algorithms, and systems fundamentals.
- Comfortable with Git, code review, and writing tests.
- Bachelor's degree or higher in AI, Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related technical field - or equivalent practical experience.
**Strong Plus**

- Hands-on experience with agent orchestration frameworks such as LangGraph, or equivalent - understanding how to structure multi-step agent workflows, not just single prompt calls.
- Any experience thinking about how to measure LLM output quality - if you have wondered how you would know whether an AI answer is actually good, and tried to do something about it, we want to hear about it. This is rare at any level and we value it highly.
- Familiarity with LLM tracing and observability tooling - for example Langfuse or similar.
- Experience with vector search or pgvector.
- Exposure to model routing across multiple providers, or multi-model cross-examination flows.
- Awareness of durable workflow engines such as Temporal, for long-running or multi-stage processes.
- TypeScript - useful for working across service boundaries and reading the front end.
- Go - useful if we need performance-critical services.
- Interest in or exposure to fintech, or data-intensive systems.
- Having shipped a consumer-facing (2C) app or agent, even as a side project.
**Mindset**

- Builder's mindset - energised by creating things that do not yet exist, not by maintaining what already does.
- Fast learner. This space moves quickly, and we care more about how quickly you pick things up than what you already know.
- High standards for credibility and reliability, and the discipline to hold that bar under time pressure.
- You take ownership of what you build, from design through deployment.
- Clear communicator - you can hold a meaningful conversation with a wealth professional, a product manager, and a fellow engineer in the same meeting.
- Comfortable with ambiguity - A2B2 is early stage, the role will evolve, and you see that as the opportunity.
- Interest in wealth, investing, or fintech is a strong plus; deep domain expertise is not required.
